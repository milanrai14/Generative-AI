# Basketball Summary

Basketball is a popular team sport played between two teams of five players each on a rectangular court. The main objective is to score points by throwing the ball through the opponent's hoop while preventing the other team from scoring.

Players move the ball by dribbling, passing, and shooting. A successful shot inside the three-point line usually earns two points, a shot beyond the three-point line earns three points, and a free throw earns one point.

Basketball requires several important skills, including dribbling, shooting, passing, rebounding, defending, speed, and coordination. Common player positions include point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward, and center.

Professional basketball is played in leagues around the world. The National Basketball Association (NBA) is one of the most famous professional leagues. Major international competitions include the FIBA Basketball World Cup and the Olympic basketball tournament.

Basketball improves physical fitness, teamwork, communication, concentration, and decision-making. It also teaches discipline, leadership, respect, and sportsmanship. Millions of people enjoy playing and watching basketball worldwide.
